# SEO proving slice (Bangkok): scope brief

Save to: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-seo-slice-brief.md`. Feeds: `/scope`, then `/architect` live. Source plan: `atlas-seo-proving-slice-plan.md` (Roy's working copy, not in the repo). Where this brief and the plan differ, this brief wins (it is later and includes Step 0). Tickets: parent BRI-114 (EP-9 SEO surface); related BRI-205, BRI-152, BRI-207, BRI-253, BRI-254 (blocks wider launch, not this slice).

## 1. Goal

Test, with real pages in Google, whether Atlas can win on queries where demand exists, the results page is weak, and we add curation with visible logic, facts computed across tours, and a distinctive house voice. About 13 Bangkok pages plus one stats page, indexed while the rest of the site stays out of the index, measured in Search Console. This is a test of the approach, not a launch.

## 2. Success measures (fixed before launch)

Checkpoints at weeks 2, 4, 8 and 12 after launch: pages discovered and crawled (week 2); at least 50% indexed is a pass, 80% good (week 4); impressions for target queries (week 8); any target query in the top 20 and any click-outs to booking (week 12). Two control pages target queries with no OTA ranking, so they separate "weak pages" from "no authority". Recorded in advance: with outreach parked, impressions on the competitive pages at week 8 are unlikely and say little about quality; the controls are the quality signal.

## 3. In scope

1. **The page list.** One "things to do in Bangkok" hub, about 8 category hubs, about 4 long-tail pages of which 2 are controls, plus one stats page. Chosen by a written method (combined US+UK+AU volume of 100 or more or ranked by two or more OTAs; at least 10 matching active tours; not a brand or bare place name; open results page and best OTA below position 3; controls exempt from the OTA rule), from files already on disk. Roy approves the ranked list before anything is generated.
2. **Snapshots.** Computed facts and the pick list are generated into committed, dated snapshot files from prod, read-only (dev lacks the category backfill). Pages are statically generated from the snapshots, never from live database reads at build time.
3. **Hub copy** generated by the content engine in the house voice (Attenborough register: calm, specific, restrained; open on one checkable fact; state the curation logic), grounded only in the snapshot and cited public sources, stored as committed files (no schema change unless `/architect` finds a reason).
4. **A rating-free hub card** with no price, availability or cancellation, and a click-out through `/go/` with a per-page campaign tag (today the campaign tag is a constant and the click ledger has no campaign column).
5. **Gate allowlist** for the slice URLs plus `/go/` and `/ingest`, a robots rewrite, a slice-only sitemap (with `lastModified` from the snapshot date), and tests proving nothing else leaks.
6. **Structured data:** an ItemList for the picks and breadcrumbs, with no ratings, review counts or aggregate ratings.
7. **Measurement plumbing:** the Search Console property and a weekly export, analytics IDs, an affiliate disclosure on every page.

## 4. Out of scope

Other cities; attraction and best-time pages (both came out Skip in BRI-206); AI per-tour copy; any price, cancellation or rating statistics; outreach; the Pinterest track; fixing the existing experience pages (BRI-254); a live price component; non-English pages; ISR; a CMS.

## 5. Hard constraints

- **Viator (BRI-204, 21 Sep).** AI copy is permitted if grounded strictly in standard product fields (title, description, duration, inclusions/exclusions) and pricing, availability and cancellation terms stay live and unmodified. Review and rating data must not be in server-rendered output of indexable pages. Hence: generated text never states price, availability or cancellation; no review or rating-derived content, not even a median; ratings may act only as a binary eligibility threshold (reuse the BRI-200 rule); ordering never uses ratings; stated curation logic uses static fields only (duration, inclusions, number of stops or tastings, category). We do not contact Viator; where a point is ambiguous, take the conservative reading and record the assumption.
- **F1 and the voice guide (`docs/08`).** All content is generated, eligibility-gated and checked in aggregate; no per-item human edit; no claim of human vetting; no invented first-person experience. The guide's banned patterns (em dashes beyond one per paragraph, contrastive reveals, cliches, stacked intensifiers) are blocking checks for hubs, not just counted. Roy's review of the first batch calibrates the engine (prompt, guide, checks), never individual pages.
- **Claim tracing.** Every number, date, name and checkable claim in the output must appear in the grounding or carry a listed source URL; reuse the tour-copy `unsourced_number` and price/cancellation checks.
- **Imitation guardrails.** Original text in a style; never reproduce or paraphrase anyone's actual words; never name or imply any person.
- **Migrations.** If a schema change is needed (for example a campaign column on the click ledger), it goes through the normal pre-deploy path. It would be the first real migration since the BRI-248 fix, so confirm "Applying migration" in the pre-deploy logs and keep the manual prd migrate step for it.
- The experience pages and `/tours/*` stay gated or `noindex`, unlinked from slice pages and absent from the slice sitemap.

## 6. Known facts from Step 0 (verified in the repo and on dev)

The gate (`apps/web/src/middleware.ts`) is all-or-nothing; the existing sitemap would expose every city, experience page, search, guides and legal pages; a 3-segment hub path collides with the `destinations/[slug]/[city]` redirect stub; `/tours/*` is always `noindex`; the content engine handles single-tour paragraphs only (no hub input type, prompt, rubric or storage); there is no cookie-consent flow and PostHog replay samples about 50%. Bangkok (dev, 2,589 active tours): price and cancellation 100%, images 100%, duration 73.8%, coordinates 76.4%, rating 53.8%; no fields for start times, languages, group size or meeting point; zero published tour copy rows.

Two Step 0 reports exist on Roy's machine; their findings are summarised in this section.

## 7. Decisions already made (13 Oct)

Bangkok; voice register A (Attenborough), Drury rejected; no Viator contact; outreach parked until there are tools, stats and components to link to; Pinterest is a separate research track; no AI tour copy in the slice; price, cancellation and rating statistics held back; existing experience pages stay gated and link-free; control pages added; Vercel Pro needed at public launch (project is on Hobby, non-commercial).

## 8. Questions for `/architect`, with defaults

1. **URL path.** Default `/destinations/thailand/bangkok/{page}` if the redirect-stub collision resolves cleanly; else `/guides/bangkok/{page}` (manifest-driven, `dynamicParams=false`) with redirects planned. Cost both.
2. **Storage of hub copy.** Default committed JSON or MD files with a grounding hash, no schema change.
3. **Campaign tag.** Default: smallest change that records a per-page campaign on `/go/` clicks (a nullable column means a migration; check whether an existing field can carry it first).
4. **Snapshot refresh.** Default manual or scheduled regeneration; each page shows "computed on <date>".
5. **Generator model.** Default the existing generator adapter with a Sonnet-class model (cost well under $2 for about 14 pages); the cheaper-model bake-off is a separate pending track.
6. **Calibration thresholds.** Default: the first batch fails if more than 3 of 14 intros fail a deterministic check or Roy's read; change prompt, guide or checks (never pages) and regenerate at most twice; if more than 2 still fail, stop and decide on a documented human-pass exception to F1.
7. **Control pages.** Default: queries with some demand, at least 10 matching tours, and no OTA ranking, from the Semrush seed leftovers.
8. **Tour eligibility in hubs.** Default: reuse the BRI-200 gate as a binary filter; ordering by static fields only.
9. **Consent and privacy.** A launch prerequisite, handled as its own task before public launch.
10. **Search Console** verification method and weekly export format.

## 9. Acceptance criteria (testable)

- With the allowlist active and the gate on, an anonymous visitor reaches only the slice URLs, `/go/` and `/ingest`; every other path still shows the gate. A test enumerates the sitemap and asserts it lists the slice pages only.
- No slice page's server HTML or JSON-LD contains a rating, review count, aggregate rating, price, availability or cancellation term (a test greps the rendered output).
- Every generated sentence passes the blocking voice checks and claim tracing; the first-batch calibration result is recorded.
- Each page's snapshot file carries its computed-on date and a grounding hash, and the page shows the date.
- Each booking click records its page's campaign tag.
- No slice page links to an experience page or a `/tours/` page except through `/go/`.

## 10. Risks

A constructed voice with no human edit may not carry the weight of firsthand experience with Google; a new domain has no authority and outreach is parked; the conservative Viator reading makes the pages thinner than competitors' (no price, no ratings); the stats page is a weak linkable asset without price data; 13 pages is a small sample.
